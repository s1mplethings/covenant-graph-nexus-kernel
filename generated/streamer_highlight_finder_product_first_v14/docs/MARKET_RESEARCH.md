# Market Research and Positioning Document

## Introduction
This document outlines the market research and positioning strategy for a tool designed to identify, score, and export high-impact moments from game streams. The goal is to address unmet needs in the live streaming and content creation industry by providing an efficient, customizable, and user-friendly solution.

---

## Alternative Solution Categories
### Manual Review and Editing
- **Description**: Streamers and editors manually review hours of footage to identify highlight-worthy moments.
- **Advantages**: Full control over the selection process.
- **Disadvantages**: Extremely time-consuming and prone to human error.

### Basic Clip Creation Tools
- **Description**: Tools like Streamlabs and OBS Studio allow users to create clips during live streams.
- **Advantages**: Easy to use and integrated with streaming platforms.
- **Disadvantages**: Lack of automation and advanced scoring capabilities.

### AI-Powered Highlight Detection
- **Description**: Platforms like Eklipse.gg use AI to detect highlights based on predefined criteria.
- **Advantages**: Automation reduces manual effort and speeds up the process.
- **Disadvantages**: Limited customization and focus on specific games.

---

## Likely Competing Workflows/Tools
1. **Streamlabs**: Offers basic clip creation tools but lacks advanced scoring and sorting features.
2. **OBS Studio**: Provides customizable recording and streaming options but requires manual effort for highlight detection.
3. **Eklipse.gg**: Uses AI for highlight detection but focuses on specific games and lacks customization options.
4. **Manual Editing Software**: Tools like Adobe Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro are used for manual editing but are time-intensive.

---

## Unmet Needs
1. **Automated Scoring and Sorting**: Current tools lack robust AI-driven scoring mechanisms to identify high-impact moments.
2. **Customization**: Users need the ability to tailor scoring criteria and sorting options to their preferences.
3. **Efficiency**: Manual review processes are inefficient and time-consuming.
4. **Integration**: Limited options for exporting clips directly to social media platforms.
5. **User-Friendly Interface**: Many tools are either too basic or overly complex, making them inaccessible to non-technical users.

---

## Differentiation
### Key Differentiators
1. **Customizable Scoring**: Allow users to define scoring criteria based on intensity, chat rate, keywords, and other metrics.
2. **Modular Interface**: A clear and intuitive GUI designed for streamers, editors, and platform managers.
3. **Export Flexibility**: Support for exporting clips in formats optimized for various social media platforms.
4. **Local Data Storage**: Initial use of browser local storage for simplicity, with options to upgrade to databases later.
5. **Manual Editing Integration**: Combine automated detection with manual tagging and editing for greater control.

### Unique Value Proposition
Our tool combines the efficiency of AI-driven highlight detection with the flexibility of manual editing, all within a user-friendly interface. It empowers streamers and editors to produce high-quality content quickly and easily.

---

## Willingness-to-Use Assumptions
1. **Streamers**: Likely to adopt the tool if it saves time and improves content quality without requiring technical expertise.
2. **Editors**: Willing to use the tool if it streamlines their workflow and integrates well with existing editing software.
3. **Platform Managers**: Interested in tools that enhance streamer engagement and retention while supporting monetization strategies.

---

## Adoption Risks
1. **Accuracy Concerns**: Users may be skeptical of the AI model's ability to accurately detect highlights.
2. **Learning Curve**: Some users may find the interface or customization options overwhelming.
3. **Compatibility Issues**: Integration with existing workflows and tools may pose challenges.
4. **Data Privacy**: Users may be hesitant to trust the tool with sensitive stream data.

---

## Validation Questions
1. **Highlight Detection Accuracy**: How well does the AI model identify high-impact moments compared to manual review?
2. **User Experience**: Is the interface intuitive and easy to navigate for non-technical users?
3. **Customization**: Do users find the scoring and sorting options flexible enough to meet their needs?
4. **Efficiency**: How much time does the tool save compared to manual review processes?
5. **Export Functionality**: Are the exported clips optimized for social media platforms and free of errors?
6. **Adoption Barriers**: What are the primary concerns or hesitations users have about adopting the tool?

---

## Initial Build Constraints
### Focus Areas
1. **Highlight Detection**: Develop a robust AI model for scoring and sorting stream events.
2. **User Interface**: Create a modular and intuitive GUI with clear ownership of controls.
3. **Data Import and Export**: Implement functionality for importing stream data and exporting filtered results.
4. **Customization**: Allow users to define scoring criteria and apply filters to the dataset.

### Exclusions
1. **Advanced Database Integration**: Start with browser local storage and defer database upgrades to later stages.
2. **Complex Social Media Integration**: Focus on exporting clips in standard formats initially, with platform-specific optimizations as a future enhancement.

---

## Conclusion
The proposed tool addresses critical unmet needs in the live streaming and content creation industry by combining AI-driven automation with user-friendly customization options. By focusing on highlight detection, modular design, and efficient workflows, the tool is positioned to become a valuable asset for streamers, editors, and platform managers. Initial development will prioritize core functionalities, with scalability and advanced features planned for future iterations.
