# Product-First User Analysis Document: Game Streamers, Editors, and Livestream Operations Teams

## Target Personas

### 1. **Game Streamers**
   - **Description**: Content creators who livestream gaming sessions on platforms like Twitch, YouTube Gaming, or Facebook Gaming. They aim to grow their audience, engage viewers, and monetize their content.
   - **Primary Needs**:
     - Automated identification of high-engagement moments during streams.
     - Tools to filter and sort highlights by game, category, or engagement score.
     - Ability to export highlight lists for further editing or sharing on social media.
   - **Pain Points**:
     - Reviewing hours of footage manually to find engaging moments is time-consuming and tedious.
     - Difficulty in identifying which moments resonate most with their audience.
     - Lack of tools to analyze audience engagement metrics (e.g., chat activity, viewer spikes).
   - **Decision Criteria**:
     - Accuracy of automated highlight detection.
     - Ease of use and minimal learning curve.
     - Integration with existing platforms and workflows.
   - **User Success Metrics**:
     - Reduction in time spent identifying highlights.
     - Increase in audience engagement metrics (e.g., likes, shares, comments) on highlight reels.
     - Growth in follower count and stream viewership.

### 2. **Content Editors**
   - **Description**: Professionals or hobbyists who assist streamers in creating polished highlight reels for platforms like YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.
   - **Primary Needs**:
     - Efficient tools to locate and extract key moments from streams.
     - Support for manual tagging and adjustments to refine automated selections.
     - Export options compatible with video editing software.
   - **Pain Points**:
     - Lack of streamlined tools for identifying and extracting highlights.
     - Difficulty in collaborating with streamers on content selection.
     - Limited ability to customize highlight scoring or filtering criteria.
   - **Decision Criteria**:
     - Flexibility in manual adjustments and tagging.
     - Compatibility with professional video editing workflows.
     - Speed and efficiency of the highlight selection process.
   - **User Success Metrics**:
     - Faster turnaround time for creating highlight reels.
     - Improved collaboration with streamers.
     - Higher quality and relevance of final video content.

### 3. **Livestream Operations Teams**
   - **Description**: Teams or individuals responsible for analyzing streamer performance, managing content, and generating insights for platforms or agencies.
   - **Primary Needs**:
     - Data-driven insights into streamer performance and audience engagement.
     - Exportable reports and highlight data for internal use or client presentations.
     - Tools to analyze trends across multiple streams or streamers.
   - **Pain Points**:
     - Limited access to actionable insights from raw footage.
     - Difficulty in aggregating and analyzing data across multiple streams.
     - Lack of tools tailored to gaming-specific metrics (e.g., in-game events, chat activity).
   - **Decision Criteria**:
     - Depth and granularity of analytics provided.
     - Ability to export data in formats compatible with reporting tools.
     - Scalability for analyzing multiple streams or streamers.
   - **User Success Metrics**:
     - Improved accuracy and depth of performance reports.
     - Increased efficiency in generating insights.
     - Enhanced decision-making based on data-driven recommendations.

## High-Frequency Workflow

### 1. **Streamers**
   - Import stream data from a platform (e.g., Twitch, YouTube Gaming).
   - Review automatically detected highlights.
   - Filter and sort highlights by game, category, or score.
   - Manually tag or adjust highlights as needed.
   - Export highlight lists for sharing or editing.

### 2. **Content Editors**
   - Receive stream data or highlight lists from streamers.
   - Refine highlights using manual tagging and adjustments.
   - Export refined highlights for use in video editing software.
   - Collaborate with streamers to finalize content.

### 3. **Livestream Operations Teams**
   - Import stream data for multiple streamers or sessions.
   - Analyze highlights and engagement metrics.
   - Generate reports or presentations based on insights.
   - Share findings with stakeholders or clients.

## Pain Points

- **Streamers**:
  - Time-consuming manual review of footage.
  - Difficulty in identifying audience engagement trends.
  - Limited tools for customizing highlight detection.

- **Content Editors**:
  - Inefficient workflows for locating and extracting highlights.
  - Lack of collaboration tools for working with streamers.
  - Limited customization options for highlight scoring.

- **Livestream Operations Teams**:
  - Limited access to actionable insights from raw footage.
  - Difficulty in aggregating data across multiple streams.
  - Lack of tools tailored to gaming-specific metrics.

## Decision Criteria

- **Accuracy**: How well does the tool identify engaging moments?
- **Efficiency**: How much time does the tool save compared to manual workflows?
- **Customization**: Can users tailor the scoring and filtering criteria to their needs?
- **Integration**: Does the tool integrate with existing platforms and workflows?
- **Scalability**: Can the tool handle multiple streams or streamers?
