# Product-First User Analysis Document: Game Stream Highlight Tool

## Target Personas

### 1. **Streamers**
   - **Description**: Content creators who stream games and aim to identify their most engaging moments to create highlight reels or improve their content strategy.
   - **Jobs-to-be-Done**:
     - Automatically detect high-engagement moments in streams.
     - Filter and sort highlights by game, category, or score.
     - Manually adjust or mark moments for fine-tuning.
   - **Pain Points**:
     - Reviewing hours of footage manually is time-consuming.
     - Difficulty identifying moments that resonate most with their audience.
   - **Decision Criteria**:
     - Accuracy of highlight detection.
     - Ease of use and time-saving features.
     - Customization options for manual adjustments.
   - **User Success Metrics**:
     - Reduction in time spent creating highlight reels.
     - Increase in audience engagement metrics (e.g., views, likes, shares).
   - **GUI Must Make Obvious**:
     - Detected highlights with scores.
     - Filtering and sorting options.
     - Manual adjustment tools.

### 2. **Content Editors**
   - **Description**: Professionals or assistants who help streamers create polished highlight reels.
   - **Jobs-to-be-Done**:
     - Efficiently extract and organize highlight moments.
     - Export highlight lists or clips for further editing in professional software.
   - **Pain Points**:
     - Lack of streamlined workflows for processing large volumes of stream data.
   - **Decision Criteria**:
     - Workflow efficiency and batch processing capabilities.
     - Compatibility with professional editing tools.
   - **User Success Metrics**:
     - Faster turnaround time for creating highlight reels.
     - Improved collaboration with streamers.
   - **GUI Must Make Obvious**:
     - Batch processing options.
     - Export functionality with clear format options.

### 3. **Livestream Operations Teams (Platform Managers)**
   - **Description**: Managers of streaming platforms or agencies who analyze trends and optimize content.
   - **Jobs-to-be-Done**:
     - Aggregate data on high-performing moments across multiple streamers.
     - Gain insights into audience engagement patterns.
   - **Pain Points**:
     - Limited tools for large-scale analysis of stream data.
   - **Decision Criteria**:
     - Scalability for analyzing multiple streams.
     - Advanced analytics and reporting features.
   - **User Success Metrics**:
     - Improved content strategy based on data insights.
     - Increased platform engagement metrics.
   - **GUI Must Make Obvious**:
     - Aggregated data views.
     - Analytics dashboards with engagement trends.

---

## High-Frequency Workflow

### 1. **Streamers**
   - **Task**: Import stream data.
     - Navigate to the dashboard.
     - Click "Import Stream" and select a platform.
     - Authenticate and select a stream to import.
   - **Task**: Edit highlights.
     - Open a stream in the Highlight Editor.
     - Review detected highlights on the timeline.
     - Adjust or mark highlights as needed.
   - **Task**: Export highlights.
     - Navigate to the Export Screen.
     - Select highlights to include.
     - Choose export format and download.

### 2. **Content Editors**
   - **Task**: Batch process highlights.
     - Import multiple streams.
     - Use filters to identify key moments.
     - Export highlight lists or clips for editing.

### 3. **Livestream Operations Teams**
   - **Task**: Analyze trends.
     - Import data from multiple streamers.
     - Use analytics tools to identify engagement patterns.
     - Export reports for strategic planning.

## Pain Points

### Streamers
   - Time-consuming manual review of footage.
   - Lack of tools to identify audience-resonating moments.

### Content Editors

   - Limited export options for professional editing.

### Livestream Operations Teams

   - Lack of actionable insights from engagement data.
