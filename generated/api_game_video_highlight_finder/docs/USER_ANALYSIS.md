# User Analysis Document for Streamer Highlight Tool

## Target Personas

### 1. Game Streamers
- **Demographics**: Typically aged between 18-35, tech-savvy, and engaged in gaming culture.
- **Goals**: To create engaging content that attracts viewers and increases their follower base.
- **Behaviors**: Streamers often record long gameplay sessions and need to extract highlight clips quickly to share on social media or during live streams.

### 2. Content Editors
- **Demographics**: Often professionals or freelancers, aged 20-40, with a background in video editing or content creation.
- **Goals**: To efficiently edit and produce high-quality video content from raw footage.
- **Behaviors**: Editors require tools that allow for quick identification of key moments in videos, enabling them to focus on creative aspects rather than tedious manual searching.

### 3. Livestream Operations Teams
- **Demographics**: Composed of individuals aged 25-45, often with experience in media production or event management.
- **Goals**: To ensure smooth operation of live events, including managing highlight clips for immediate sharing.
- **Behaviors**: These teams need reliable tools that integrate seamlessly into their workflow, allowing for real-time clip extraction and management.

## Jobs-to-be-Done

1. **Identify Highlights**: Automatically detect and mark highlight segments in gameplay videos.
2. **Manage Clips**: Provide functionalities to add, edit, delete, and organize highlight clips.
3. **Export Clips**: Generate downloadable lists of highlight clips for easy sharing across platforms.
4. **Analyze Performance**: Offer basic statistics on highlight performance to inform future content strategies.

## High-Frequency Workflow

1. **Upload Video**: Users upload a single gameplay video file to the tool.
2. **Highlight Detection**: The tool processes the video to identify potential highlight segments.
3. **Review Highlights**: Users review the detected highlights, making adjustments as necessary (add, edit, delete).
4. **Export Clips**: Users generate a downloadable list of clips for sharing or further editing.
5. **Analyze Data**: Users review statistics related to the highlights to gauge viewer engagement and performance.

## Pain Points

- **Time Consumption**: Manually searching through long gameplay videos is tedious and time-consuming.
- **Inaccuracy**: Existing tools may not accurately detect highlights, leading to frustration and wasted effort.
- **Integration Issues**: Difficulty in integrating highlight tools with existing workflows or platforms can hinder productivity.
- **Lack of Analytics**: Limited insights into highlight performance can prevent users from optimizing their content strategy.

## Decision Criteria

1. **Ease of Use**: The tool must have an intuitive interface that allows users to quickly navigate and utilize its features.
2. **Accuracy of Highlight Detection**: Users prioritize tools that reliably identify key moments in gameplay.
3. **Export Options**: The ability to easily export clips in various formats is crucial for sharing content.
4. **Integration Capabilities**: The tool should work seamlessly with other platforms and tools used by streamers and editors.
5. **Support and Documentation**: Comprehensive support and clear documentation are essential for troubleshooting and maximizing tool usage.

## User Success Metrics

- **Time Saved**: Measure the reduction in time taken to identify and export highlight clips compared to manual methods.
- **User Satisfaction**: Gather feedback through surveys to assess user satisfaction with the tool's features and performance.
- **Clip Engagement**: Analyze viewer engagement metrics on exported clips to determine the effectiveness of the highlights.
- **Adoption Rate**: Track the number of users adopting the tool over time as a measure of its success in the market.

## GUI Must Make Obvious

1. **Highlight Detection Status**: Clearly indicate whether the highlight detection process is ongoing or completed.
2. **Clip Management Options**: Provide obvious buttons for adding, editing, and deleting clips, ensuring users can manage their highlights effortlessly.
3. **Export Functionality**: Make the export options prominent, allowing users to quickly download their highlight lists.
4. **Statistics Overview**: Display key statistics related to highlights in an easily accessible format, helping users make informed decisions.
5. **User Guidance**: Include tooltips or a help section that guides users through the features and functionalities of the tool.

## Conclusion

The Streamer Highlight Tool is designed to address the specific needs of game streamers, content editors, and livestream operations teams. By focusing on their workflows, pain points, and decision criteria, the tool can provide a seamless experience that enhances productivity and content quality. The user interface must prioritize clarity and ease of use, ensuring that users can efficiently manage their highlight clips and derive valuable insights from their content. This user analysis serves as a foundational guide for the development of the tool, ensuring that it meets the needs of its target audience effectively.
