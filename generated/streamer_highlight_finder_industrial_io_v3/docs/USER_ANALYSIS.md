# Product-First User Analysis Document: Game Stream Highlight Analyzer

## Target Personas

### Game Streamers
**Needs:**
- Efficiently identify high-impact moments in their streams.
- Improve content quality and engagement by showcasing highlights.
- Save time on manual editing and highlight discovery.

**Pain Points:**
- Manually reviewing hours of footage is time-consuming.
- Difficulty in identifying moments that resonate with audiences.
- Lack of tools to integrate multiple data sources (e.g., chat logs, metadata).

**Decision Criteria:**
- Automation of highlight detection to reduce manual effort.
- Integration of multi-source data (chat logs, viewer spikes, metadata).
- Customizable scoring and filtering to align with audience preferences.

**User Success Metrics:**
- Reduction in time spent identifying highlights.
- Increase in audience engagement metrics (likes, shares, comments).
- Improved content quality and frequency of highlight uploads.

**GUI Must Make Obvious:**
- Importing stream data from multiple sources.
- Visualizing high-impact moments with clear indicators.
- Filtering and sorting highlights based on user-defined criteria.

---

### Content Editors
**Needs:**
- Streamline the process of creating highlight reels.
- Access tools that allow for quick filtering and scoring of moments.
- Collaborate with streamers on finalizing highlight selections.

**Pain Points:**
- Limited tools for automating highlight discovery.
- Difficulty in aligning with streamer preferences for highlight selection.
- Challenges in managing large volumes of video and metadata.

**Decision Criteria:**
- Tools for efficient filtering and sorting of highlights.
- Collaboration features for sharing and reviewing highlight selections.
- Export functionality for generating ready-to-use highlight lists.

**User Success Metrics:**
- Reduction in editing time per highlight reel.
- Improved alignment with streamer preferences.
- Increased output of high-quality highlight reels.

**GUI Must Make Obvious:**
- Filtering and sorting controls for highlight selection.
- Manual annotation tools for adjusting highlight tags.
- Export options for generating downloadable highlight lists.

---

### Livestream Operations Teams
**Needs:**
- Enhance platform engagement by promoting high-quality content.
- Provide tools for streamers to improve their content.
- Analyze trends in high-performing moments across games and streamers.

**Pain Points:**
- Lack of insights into what makes content engaging.
- Difficulty in scaling highlight discovery across thousands of streams.
- Limited tools for integrating platform data into highlight workflows.

**Decision Criteria:**
- Scalable tools for analyzing and scoring highlights across multiple streams.
- Integration with platform-specific data (e.g., viewer spikes, chat sentiment).
- Reporting features for analyzing trends and engagement metrics.

**User Success Metrics:**
- Increase in platform engagement metrics (watch time, retention).
- Improved streamer satisfaction with platform-provided tools.
- Identification of trends in high-performing content.

**GUI Must Make Obvious:**
- Scoring and ranking of highlights across multiple streams.
- Reporting tools for analyzing trends and engagement metrics.
- Integration of platform-specific data into workflows.

---

## Jobs-to-Be-Done

1. **Game Streamers:**
   - Automatically detect and score high-impact moments in streams.
   - Filter and sort highlights based on audience engagement metrics.
   - Export highlight lists for sharing on social media or editing platforms.

2. **Content Editors:**
   - Import and process multi-source data to identify potential highlights.
   - Collaborate with streamers to finalize highlight selections.
   - Export polished highlight reels for distribution.

3. **Livestream Operations Teams:**
   - Analyze trends in high-performing moments across streams.
   - Provide tools for streamers to improve content quality.
   - Promote high-quality content to increase platform engagement.

---

## High-Frequency Workflow

1. **Import Data:**
   - Users import industrial input bundles containing platform logs, chat data, video metadata, manual annotations, and operational reviews.

2. **Score Highlights:**
   - The system analyzes multi-source data to score and rank moments based on predefined criteria (e.g., viewer spikes, chat sentiment, intensity).

3. **Filter and Sort:**
   - Users apply filters to narrow down highlights by game, streamer, category, and score.

4. **Annotate Highlights:**
   - Users manually adjust highlight tags or add new items to the dataset.

5. **Export Results:**
   - Users generate downloadable highlight lists and clips based on selected criteria.
