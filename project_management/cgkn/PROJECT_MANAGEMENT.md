## Active risks

- `RK-001` Project management becomes documentation overhead instead of improving delivery. — Tailor artifacts by uncertainty and criticality; require each artifact to support a decision, quality check, or handoff.
- `RK-002` A management helper accidentally becomes a second generation mainline. — Keep management artifacts as bounded context inside the existing provider route and register them as non-formal support artifacts.
- `RK-003` Enterprise experience becomes an unsearchable or self-confirming archive. — Require applicability, evidence, bounded retrieval, usage receipts, and retirement or supersession.
